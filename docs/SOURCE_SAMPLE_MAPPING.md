# Source Sample Mapping

The attached sample files were used as the business-shape reference for the MVP.

## Maintenance Bill

Source concepts:
- Society name and registration number
- Address
- Unit number
- Unit area
- Unit type
- Member / joint member
- Bill month
- Wing / floor
- Bill number
- Bill date
- Due date
- Maintenance charges
- Water charges
- Sinking fund
- Non-occupancy charges
- 2-wheeler parking
- 4-wheeler parking
- Interest
- Adjustment
- Principal arrears
- Interest arrears
- Total payable
- Receipt details on some bills

## Collection Sheet

Source concepts:
- Bill number
- Unit number
- Member
- Bill amount
- Interest
- Arrears
- Total amount
- Amount received
- Cheque number
- Payment date
- Bank/cash
- Balance
- Interest/penalty

## Bill Register

Source concepts:
- Unit number
- Maintenance
- Water
- Sinking Fund
- Non Occupancy
- 2 Wheeler Parking
- 4 Wheeler Parking
- Amount
- Interest
- Bill Amount
- Principal Arrear
- Interest Arrear
- Payable Amount
- Balance Amount

## Design rule

These documents are operating records used to reproduce the current workflow. They are not themselves the legal source for future statutory configuration.
